# PRM code reference

Codes 1–41 for the `PRM_Code` variable in the Patient-Reported Measures Survey and Survey Response tables. Each code identifies one questionnaire type and subtype combination. Code values and questionnaire mappings retain their documented spelling.

| 2 | BPI — Brief Pain Inventory | BPI-4 | Brief Pain Inventory - 4 (BPI-4) | BPI-4 is a medical survey with four questions used to measure pain, developed by the Pain Research Group of the WHO Collaborating Centre for Symptom Evaluation in Cancer Care. |
| 3 | EVS — Exercise as a Vital Sign | EVS | Exercise as a Vital Sign (EVS) | EVS is a 2-question item asked of every patient during an outpatient visit. |

**Shared PROMIS note (codes 20, 25–36):** The PROMIS is a series of self-report measures for adults and covers different functions, symptoms, behaviors and feelings in the domains of physical, mental and social health.

## Mapping caveat

Code 39 pairs subtype `PHQ-9` with a PHQ-T9 survey name. This mapping is unresolved; do not silently substitute a different code.
